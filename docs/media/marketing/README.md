# SurfMeter marketing infographic

Created 2026-11-06 using the built-in image generation tool. The portrait PNG is a marketing illustration, not an app screenshot. Usage figures are sample data: 3h 10m today versus 4h 2m yesterday, a difference of 52 minutes. The illustrated menu panel and wave mark are interpretations rather than pixel-exact reproductions of the app. No real browsing data was used.

## Research and design choices

Reviewed current infographic guidance and 2026 trend coverage. Chose focused storytelling, large typography, directly labeled charts, restrained decoration and a clear call to action. These are design choices, not evidence that one style guarantees marketing results. A static portrait format suits this shareable artifact; interactive widgets and motion can be explored as separate assets.

- [VistaPrint: infographic examples and 2026 trends](https://www.vistaprint.com/hub/infographic-examples): practical minimalism and a focused business message.
- [Canva: how to make an infographic](https://www.canva.com/learn/how-to-make-an-infographic/): visual storytelling and hierarchy.
- [NN/g: designing effective infographics](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/designing-effective-infographics/): integrating text and graphics and providing enough context for a standalone image. This is established guidance, not a new 2026 trend.
- [Venngage: infographic design trends](https://venngage.com/blog/infographic-design-trends/): typography and examples of contemporary visual storytelling. Its title refers to 2025; search metadata reports a 2026 update.

SurfMeter's existing macOS neutral palette and blue brand accent guide the treatment. Green/red comparisons also use arrows and words. The graphic makes no claims about accuracy certification, saved hours, battery use or Safari availability. The footer identifies the development alpha and required Chrome setup. Published release assets are unchanged.
